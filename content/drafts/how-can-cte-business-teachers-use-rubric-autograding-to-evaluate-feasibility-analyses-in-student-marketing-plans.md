### The Assessment Volume Reality
For a CTE educator managing three sections of Entrepreneurship, two sections of Sports & Entertainment Marketing, and advising the school's DECA chapter, grading these capstone plans is an overwhelming operational challenge:
* **Staggering Word Count:** 100 students submitting 20-page business plans generate **600,000 words** of technical writing—the equivalent of reading *War and Peace* and *Les Misérables* combined, accompanied by hundreds of complex financial spreadsheets.
* **Cognitive Load per Document:** Evaluating a feasibility study is far more complex than scoring a standard English essay. The teacher must simultaneously check **prose clarity**, verify **mathematical logic** (e.g., Does the contribution margin cover the calculated monthly fixed overhead?), audit **market research credibility** (e.g., Are the demographic numbers sourced from real local census data or fabricated?), and evaluate **strategic coherence** across all six sections.
* **Feedback Latency:** Manual line-by-line review requires **35 to 50 minutes per plan**. Across 100 students, this represents **60 to 85 hours of grading**. Consequently, graded drafts often take 3 to 4 weeks to return to students—long after the instructional unit has ended and past the registration deadlines for CTSO competitive events.

### The Downfall of Superficial Holistic Grading
Faced with grading fatigue, even dedicated teachers are forced into cognitive triage, relying on holistic scanning and generic rubric checkboxes:
* *"Strong competitive matrix, but tighten your financial projections."*
* *"Good customer persona; make sure your marketing budget aligns with revenue."*
* *"Ensure your break-even units make sense."*

These holistic comments provide zero actionable guidance for student entrepreneurs. A high school senior preparing for the DECA International Career Development Conference (ICDC) or a community college student pitching for seed capital cannot convert *"tighten your financial projections"* into an improved balance sheet. The student needs to see **the precise calculation where variable labor costs were omitted**, understand why their Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) exceeds their Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), and receive formative revision prompts grounded in their own drafted prose.

### 1. Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) Rubric Parsing
Checkmark accepts rubrics in any format: uploaded PDFs, scanned image rubrics, native LMS rubric syncs (Canvas, Buzz, Moodle), or customized CTSO rubrics (DECA Integrated Marketing Campaign, FBLA Business Financial Plan). 

The system decomposes the rubric into an **Abstract Syntax Tree (AST)**:
* **Root Node:** Assignment Metadata (e.g., *DECA Entrepreneurship Innovation Plan - 100 Points Total*).
* **Branch Nodes (Criteria):** Individual assessment dimensions (e.g., *Criterion 1: Market Analysis & Demographics - 20 pts; Criterion 2: Financial Strategy & Unit Economics - 25 pts*).
* **Leaf Nodes (Performance Levels):** Specific qualifying descriptors across achievement bands (*Exceeds Standards [23–25 pts], Meets Standards [18–22 pts], Developing [12–17 pts], Inadequate [0–11 pts]*).

### 2. Grounded Quote-Anchored Evidence Extraction
Rather than generating vague narrative summaries, Checkmark’s engine acts as an evidentiary auditor. For each criterion in the AST, the engine scans the student's submission and extracts the **exact sentences, data points, and mathematical formulas** that justify the achievement level:
* For **Market Demographics**, Checkmark isolates the specific lines citing target population figures, survey sample sizes, and income brackets.
* For **Unit Economics**, Checkmark pulls the itemized Cost of Goods Sold bill of materials and recalculates the student's break-even arithmetic to verify mathematical accuracy.
* For **Competitive Advantage**, Checkmark extracts the direct comparisons between the student's venture and identified market rivals.

If a student's business plan makes sweeping claims without evidentiary support (e.g., asserting high profit margins without providing an itemized COGS table), Checkmark explicitly flags the absence of evidence:
