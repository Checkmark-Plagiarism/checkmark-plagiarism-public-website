### 5.1 Multi-Factor Passage-Level AI Writing Detection

Rather than outputting a single, arbitrary whole-paper percentage that triggers panic and unprovable accusations, Checkmark provides **Passage-Level Granularity**:
- **Calibrated Confidence Sliders:** Specific sentences or paragraphs are underlined directly in the submission, paired with sidebar evidence cards displaying confidence ratings based on perplexity, burstiness, syntax uniformity, and vocabulary predictability.
- **Honest Short-Text Guardrails (<150 Words):** For short harmonic justifications, listening warm-ups, or single-measure error identifications under ~150 words, Checkmark automatically displays `N/A` rather than guessing on statistically insufficient sample sizes.
- **Educator-Only Flag Statuses:** Flags (*Flagged*, *Resolved*, *Not Flagged*) remain strictly private to the teacher, ensuring that preliminary algorithmic signals never result in automatic penalties or student humiliation.

### 5.2 Defensible Plagiarism & Peer Match Detection

When students share harmonic analyses across different class periods or copy solutions from online AP Music Theory forums, Checkmark delivers definitive proof:
- **Side-by-Side Source View:** Displays the student’s prose directly adjacent to the matched online source or open-access music repository, complete with clickable URLs.
- **Cohort & Cross-Period Peer Matching:** Compares submissions across all class sections within the high school without exposing student data externally. If Period 2 Band students submit identical voice-leading analyses to Period 5 Choir students, Checkmark highlights the exact shared sentences.
- **Uncited Source Differentiation:** Separates unformatted musical citations from intentional copying, allowing teachers to provide targeted citation instruction.

### 5.3 Writing Process Analysis & Patent-Pending Essay Playback™ (Flagship)

Checkmark’s flagship innovation provides the ultimate evidentiary foundation for academic integrity:
- **1x–8x Scrubbable Replay:** Watch the student’s entire composition session unfold like a high-definition video. See how melodic dictations are constructed note-by-note, track pauses during audio playbacks, and observe legitimate self-correction.
- **External Paste Buffer Inspector:** When text is pasted into the document from outside the browser, Checkmark captures and stores the raw clipboard text at that exact millisecond. Even if the student subsequently rewrites, deletes, or paraphrases every word to deceive AI detectors, the teacher can click "Jump to Playback" to view the original pasted source.
- **Transcription Detection:** Identifies unnatural, mechanical typing cadence (e.g., continuous 95 WPM typing with zero revisions), signaling that a student was manually retyping a prompt generated on an external smartphone or tablet.

### 5.4 AI Autograder with Direct LMS Passback

High school music teachers managing heavy performance schedules and large rosters need efficient grading workflows:
- **Teacher-in-the-Loop AP Rubrics:** Upload standard AP Music Theory Free-Response rubrics or build custom criteria. Checkmark’s Autograder evaluates student analyses, generating point breakdowns and quote-anchored justifications tied directly to specific measures in the student’s writeup.
- **Teacher Final Authority:** All AI evaluations remain editable draft suggestions until reviewed, modified, and approved by the instructor.
- **Direct Gradebook Synchronization:** With a single click, publish finalized scores and detailed formative feedback straight into **Canvas LMS SpeedGrader, Buzz LMS, Schoology, or Google Classroom**.

### 5.5 Privacy, FERPA/COPPA Compliance, & Enterprise Security

Checkmark protects student and school district data under strict legal and ethical standards:
- **Zero Model Training Guarantee:** Student work, scores, and musical submissions are never used to train public or proprietary AI models.
- **Full Legal Compliance:** Meets all FERPA and COPPA statutory standards, utilizing enterprise-grade encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- **Native Ecosystem Support:** Fully integrated with Google Docs on 1:1 Chromebooks, Microsoft 365, Canvas LTI 1.3, Buzz LMS, and Google/Microsoft Single Sign-On (SSO).
