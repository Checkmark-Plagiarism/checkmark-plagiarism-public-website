## 4. Step-by-Step High School Music Teacher Audit Protocol

When evaluating take-home harmonic analyses, melodic dictation writeups, or part-writing error justifications, high school AP Music Theory teachers should apply a structured, four-tier audit protocol. This protocol shifts the classroom dynamic from adversarial suspicion to objective, evidence-based coaching.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        FOUR-TIER AP MUSIC THEORY INTEGRITY AUDIT WORKFLOW                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                                        │
│   [ STEP 1: SCORE-TO-PROSE & ROMAN NUMERAL CONCORDANCE AUDIT ]                                         │
│   └─ Check assigned score against student writeup: key, clefs, inversions, and cadence points.         │
│                                │                                                                       │
│                                ▼                                                                       │
│   [ STEP 2: VOICE-LEADING & FIGURED BASS RULES SPOT-CHECK ]                                            │
│   └─ Inspect for parallel 5ths/8ves, unresolved leading tones, 7th resolutions, and improper doubling.  │
│                                │                                                                       │
│                                ▼                                                                       │
│   [ STEP 3: WRITING PROCESS & AUDITORY PAUSE REPLAY VIA ESSAY PLAYBACK™ ]                              │
│   └─ Scrub timeline at 1x–8x speed: verify 3-pass listening pauses, check clipboard paste inspector.   │
│                                │                                                                       │
│                                ▼                                                                       │
│   [ STEP 4: THE RESTORATIVE PIANO / KEYBOARD DEBRIEF ]                                                 │
│   └─ Non-punitive conference: student plays/sings outer voices, listens to harmony, rebuilds mastery. │
│                                                                                                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Step 1: Score-to-Prose & Roman Numeral Concordance Audit

Begin by cross-referencing the student’s submitted written analysis against the assigned score or audio passage:

1. **Key Signature and Tonal Center Verification:** Does the student’s analysis correctly identify minor vs. relative major? If the piece is in F# minor (3 sharps) and the writeup constantly refers to the tonic as A major without any modulation analysis, check for LLM token confusion.
2. **Clef and Pitch Integrity:** Verify whether the analysis accounts for alto or tenor clefs if assigned. Generative vision models frequently read tenor clef as treble clef, resulting in Roman numerals that are off by exactly a third or fifth.
3. **Cadence Classification Alignment:** Match the labeled cadences against the score’s terminal measures. If measure 8 ends on a clear Half Cadence (`V`) with scale degree `2` in the soprano, but the writeup asserts a Perfect Authentic Cadence (`V - I`), the student may have pasted a generalized AI response.

---

### Step 2: Voice-Leading & Figured Bass Rules Spot-Check

Perform a targeted check of the student’s analytical justifications against common-practice contrapuntal rules:

1. **Outer-Voice Counterpoint Audit:** Check the soprano and bass movement at every cadence and chord transition. If the score clearly exhibits parallel octaves between soprano and bass, but the student’s writeup describes the motion as "independent contrary motion that reinforces vocal independence," the prose is detached from musical reality.
2. **Seventh Chord Resolution Check:** Look at every labeled seventh chord (`V7`, `ii7`, `vii°7`). Does the student’s prose correctly account for the downward step resolution of the chordal 7th?
3. **Leading Tone Tracking:** In dominant harmonies (`V`, `V7`, `vii°`), verify that the leading tone resolves upward to the tonic when in an outer voice. If the writeup discusses the leading tone moving downwards to form a full triad, flag the passage for review.

---

### Step 3: Keystroke Telemetry & Auditory Pause Analysis via Essay Playback™

Open the submission in **Checkmark Plagiarism** to review the student’s drafting session in patent-pending **Essay Playback™**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   ESSAY PLAYBACK™ TELEMETRY AUDIT FOR AP MUSIC THEORY DRAFTING                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                                        │
│   TELEMETRY SIGNALS TO INSPECT AT 1x TO 8x SCRUB SPEED:                                                │
│                                                                                                        │
│   [A] DICTATION LISTENING INTERVALS:                                                                   │
│       Authentic student shows 15–30s inactive listening pauses alternating with rapid note/solfège     │
│       drafting, rhythmic adjustments, and interval backspacing.                                        │
│                                                                                                        │
│   [B] EXTERNAL PASTE BUFFER INSPECTION:                                                                │
│       Identifies if complex Roman numeral sequences (`I - vi - ii6/5 - V6/4-5/3 - I`) were pasted       │
│       instantaneously from an external clipboard. Preserves original clipboard text even if edited.    │
│                                                                                                        │
│   [C] MECHANICAL TRANSCRIPTION DETECTION:                                                             │
│       Detects steady 85–110 WPM typing with zero pauses or revisions (indicating the student is        │
│       manually copying text while looking at a phone screen or secondary device).                      │
│                                                                                                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Scrub Through the Timeline:** Use the playback slider to watch the document take shape. Authentic harmonic analysis involves recursive jumping between measures, revising chord quality labels (e.g., changing `ii` to `ii°` upon noticing a flat), and adjusting Roman numeral cases.
2. **Audit External Pastes:** Click on any highlighted paste block in the **Paste Buffer Inspector**. Checkmark captures and displays the exact string that entered the document from outside the browser session, allowing you to see if an entire AI-generated harmonic breakdown was inserted at once.
3. **Exonerate Diligent Students:** If a generic detector flagged a student’s legitimate writeup due to dense formulas (`V7/V -> V -> I`), Essay Playback™ provides clear evidence of authentic human thought—showing individual keystrokes, thoughtful pauses, and organic revision cycles.

---

### Step 4: The Restorative Piano / Keyboard Debrief

If significant discrepancies emerge between the score, the keystroke telemetry, and the written analysis, schedule a brief (5–10 minute) **Restorative Piano Debrief** during office hours, advisory period, or sectionals.

The goal of this conference is not punitive confrontation, but restorative musical growth:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     RESTORATIVE PIANO DEBRIEF: CONVERSATIONAL SCRIPT                                   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                                        │
│   "Thanks for meeting with me, Julian. I was reviewing your harmonic analysis on the Bach Chorale in   │
│   G Minor. Your writeup uses some very advanced terminology, but when I look at the score and our      │
│   writing process telemetry, there are a few interesting things I'd love to explore together.          │
│                                                                                                        │
│   Let's sit at the piano. Could you play the bass line for measures 3 and 4 while I play the soprano? │
│   Now, as we listen to that progression, what do your ears hear at the cadence? Does it feel like an   │
│   authentic cadence or a half cadence? Let's sing the leading tone together and see where it resolves.│
│                                                                                                        │
│   In your writeup, it mentions a stable I6/4 chord and smooth contrary motion, but at the keyboard, we │
│   can hear that strong dominant pull and the parallel fifths. Tell me about how you arrived at this    │
│   analysis during your drafting session."                                                             │
│                                                                                                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

By transitioning the dialogue to the acoustic keyboard, the educator removes defensiveness. A student who genuinely understands the material will quickly play, sing, or clarify their analytical intentions; a student who outsourced the work to an AI bot will immediately recognize the gap between synthetic text and auditory reality.
