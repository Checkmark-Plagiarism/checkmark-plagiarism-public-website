Your mission is to generate high-intent, high school educator-facing article topics that 9th–12th grade teachers (English/Language Arts, Social Studies/History, AP Capstone/Seminar/Lang/Lit, High School Science), department chairs, instructional technology coaches, and high school administrators actively search for when managing AI in high school writing assignments, investigating suspected plagiarism, verifying student authorship, or managing grading workloads.

## 🎯 MANDATORY AUDIENCE & CLASSROOM SETTING FOCUS

Every candidate topic must be squarely rooted in the **HIGH SCHOOL CLASSROOM SETTING (Grades 9–12)** and focused on **HIGH SCHOOL STUDENT WRITING ASSIGNMENTS**:

### Core High School Writing Assignment Types to Feature:
- **Literary Analysis & Thematic Essays**: High school ELA essays analyzing novels, plays (e.g., Shakespeare, Arthur Miller), short stories, poetry, and character development.
- **Argumentative & Persuasive Essays**: 5-paragraph to multi-page argument essays with counterarguments, thesis statements, and textual evidence.
- **Document-Based Questions (DBQs) & Synthesis Papers**: AP US History, AP World History, AP European History, and Civics essays requiring document synthesis and point-of-view analysis.
- **High School Research Papers & Annotated Bibliographies**: Multi-source research projects requiring MLA/APA citations, primary/secondary source differentiation, and patchwriting avoidance.
- **Claim-Evidence-Reasoning (CER) Lab Reports**: High school Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Environmental Science writeups requiring authentic data interpretation.
- **In-Class Timed Writes vs. Take-Home Chromebook Essays**: Timed essays written in 45–90 minute class periods vs take-home multi-draft papers written on 1:1 Chromebooks in Google Docs or Canvas LMS.
- **Personal Narratives & College Application Essays**: Reflective personal essays, college admissions personal statements, and scholarship essays.
